# Email and Collaboration Tools

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT for training. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company.

## Approved Tools

Company email, the corporate chat platform and the shared document drive are the only approved collaboration tools.

## Mailbox Limits

Each mailbox has a 50 GB storage limit. Emails older than three years are archived automatically.

## External Sharing

Documents may be shared with external parties only through expiring links that are protected by a password.
