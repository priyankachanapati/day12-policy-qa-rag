# Public Holidays and Special Leave

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT for training. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company.

## Public Holidays

Brightlake Systems publishes a list of 12 public holidays each December for the following year.

## Working on a Holiday

Employees who work on a public holiday receive one compensatory day off, to be taken within 60 days.

## Bereavement Leave

Up to 5 working days of paid bereavement leave are available after the death of an immediate family member.
