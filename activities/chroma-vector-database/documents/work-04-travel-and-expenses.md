# Business Travel and Expenses

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT for training. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company.

## Booking Travel

Flights under 6 hours are booked in economy class. Hotels are reimbursed up to USD 180 per night.

## Meals

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to USD 60 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Submitting Claims

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days with receipts for any item above USD 25.
