# Health and Safety

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT for training. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company.

## Fire Safety

Fire drills take place every six months. Employees must leave by the nearest exit and gather at the car park assembly point.

## Accidents

All workplace accidents and near misses must be reported to the facilities team on the same day.

## Ergonomics

Employees can request an ergonomic assessment and equipment such as a footrest or monitor arm.
