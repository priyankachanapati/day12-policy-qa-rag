# Performance Reviews

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT for training. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company.

## Review Cycle

Formal performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Each review compares results against goals agreed at the start of the cycle.

## Ratings

Managers use a five-point rating scale from 'needs improvement' to 'exceptional'. Ratings are calibrated across each department before they are shared.

## Development Plans

Every review ends with a development plan containing at least two learning goals. Progress on the plan is discussed in monthly one-to-one meetings.
