# Office Guidelines

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT for training. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company.

## Desk Booking

Desks in the office are booked through the Workplace app up to two weeks in advance.

## Clean Desk

Confidential papers must be locked away at the end of each day, and screens must be locked when unattended.

## Meeting Rooms

Meeting rooms may be booked for a maximum of two hours unless approved by the office manager.
