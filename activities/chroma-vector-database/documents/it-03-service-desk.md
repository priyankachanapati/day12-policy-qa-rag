# IT Service Desk

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT for training. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company.

## Contact

The IT Service Desk is available on extension 4357 or servicedesk@brightlake.example from 08:00 to 18:00 on working days.

## Ticket Priorities

Priority 1 incidents, such as a full outage, receive a response within 30 minutes. Standard requests are answered within one working day.

## Out of Hours

Outside working hours, only priority 1 incidents are handled, through the on-call phone line.
