# Code of Conduct

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT for training. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company.

## Purpose

The code of conduct sets the standard of behaviour expected from every employee. It applies in the office, at client sites and in online meetings.

## Respect at Work

Employees must treat colleagues, customers and suppliers with courtesy and respect. Harassment, bullying and discrimination are not tolerated in any form.

## Reporting Concerns

Concerns can be reported to a line manager, to HR at hr-ops@brightlake.example, or anonymously through the Speak Up portal. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is prohibited.
