# Salary and Payroll

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT for training. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company.

## Pay Date

Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month by bank transfer. Payslips are available in the HR Portal two days before the pay date.

## Salary Reviews

Salaries are reviewed once a year in March, taking into account performance ratings and market data.

## Payroll Errors

Employees who notice an error on their payslip should contact payroll@brightlake.example within 10 working days. Corrections are made in the next payroll run.
