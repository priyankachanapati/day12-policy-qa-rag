# Parental Leave

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT for training. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company.

## Maternity Leave

Birth mothers are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave after at least 80 days of service in the previous 12 months.

## Paternity Leave

Fathers and secondary caregivers receive 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the birth.

## Returning to Work

Employees returning from parental leave may work reduced hours for the first four weeks by agreement with their manager.
