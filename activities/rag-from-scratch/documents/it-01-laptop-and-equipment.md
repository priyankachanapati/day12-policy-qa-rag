# Laptop and Equipment

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT for training. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company.

## Issued Equipment

Every employee receives a company laptop on their first day. Laptops are replaced every 4 years or earlier if they cannot be repaired.

## Lost or Stolen Devices

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours. Stolen devices also require a police report.

## Returning Equipment

Departing employees must return all equipment within 5 working days of their last day.
