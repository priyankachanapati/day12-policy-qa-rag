# Annual Leave

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT for training. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis.

## How to Request

Annual leave is requested in the HR Portal at least 7 calendar days in advance and must be approved by the line manager.

## Carry Forward

Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next year and must be used by 31 March.
