# Multi-Factor Authentication

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT for training. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company.

## Requirement

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every account, including email, VPN and cloud tools.

## Approved Method

The Brightlake Authenticator app is the approved second factor. SMS codes are not accepted.

## Lost Phone

If the phone with the authenticator app is lost, contact the IT Service Desk immediately to reset MFA.
