# Learning and Training Budget

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT for training. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company.

## Annual Budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of USD 1,000 for courses, certifications and conferences.

## Approval

Training requests must be approved by the line manager in the Learning Portal at least three weeks before the course starts.

## Repayment Rule

Employees who leave within six months of a company-funded certification must repay 50 percent of its cost.
