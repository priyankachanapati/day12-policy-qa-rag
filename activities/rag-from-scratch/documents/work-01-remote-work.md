# Remote Work

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT for training. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company.

## Eligibility

Employees may work from home after completing their probation period, if their role is marked hybrid-eligible.

## Remote Days

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to 2 days per week. Requests are made in the HR Portal by Friday of the previous week.

## Core Hours

Remote workers must be available online between 10:00 and 16:00 local time and must use the company VPN.
