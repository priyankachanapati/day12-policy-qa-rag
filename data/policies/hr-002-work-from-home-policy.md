# Work-From-Home Policy (HR-002)

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE POLICY. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company. This document was created for a training exercise and does not describe any real organisation.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy sets out when and how employees of Brightlake Systems may work from home. It applies to all permanent employees whose role has been designated "hybrid-eligible" by their department head.

## 2. Eligibility

Employees become eligible to work from home after successfully completing their probation period. Employees still on probation must work from the office unless their department head grants a written exception.

## 3. Number of Work-From-Home Days

Eligible employees may work from home for up to 2 days per week. Work-from-home days cannot be accumulated or carried over to a later week.

## 4. Requesting Work-From-Home Days

Work-from-home days must be requested in the HR Portal by 17:00 on the Friday of the preceding week. The line manager approves or declines each request. Managers may decline requests during critical project phases, client visits, or mandatory in-office events.

## 5. Core Working Hours

Employees working from home must be available online between the core hours of 10:00 and 16:00 local time. During core hours employees must respond to messages within 30 minutes and keep their calendar status up to date.

## 6. Home Workspace and Security

Employees must work from a quiet, private space where confidential information cannot be seen or overheard. Employees must connect to company systems through the company VPN at all times when working from home. Use of public Wi-Fi networks for work is permitted only when connected through the company VPN.

## 7. Internet Allowance

Hybrid-eligible employees receive a monthly internet allowance of USD 40, paid automatically through payroll. No receipts are required for the internet allowance.

## 8. Working From Another Country

Working remotely from a country other than the employee's country of employment requires prior written approval from HR and is limited to a maximum of 30 calendar days per calendar year.

## 9. Contact

Questions about this policy should be directed to the HR Operations team at hr-ops@brightlake.example.
