# Password and Account Security Policy (IT-001)

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE POLICY. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company. This document was created for a training exercise and does not describe any real organisation.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines the minimum requirements for passwords and account security for every Brightlake Systems user account, including employees, interns and contractors.

## 2. Password Requirements

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords must not contain the user's name, username, or the word "Brightlake". Users must not reuse any of their previous 10 passwords.

Passwords must be changed every 180 days, and immediately if there is any suspicion that a password has been compromised.

## 3. Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all accounts. Users must use the Brightlake Authenticator mobile app as their second factor. SMS codes are not an approved MFA method.

## 4. Account Lockout

An account is automatically locked for 15 minutes after 5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts. If the account is locked again within 24 hours, it remains locked until it is unlocked by the IT Service Desk.

## 5. Password Storage and Sharing

Passwords must never be shared with anyone, including colleagues, managers, or IT staff. Passwords must not be written down or stored in plain-text files. The only approved password manager is KeyVault, which is installed on all company laptops.

## 6. Reporting a Compromised Account

If a user suspects that their password or account has been compromised, they must report it to the IT Service Desk within 1 hour of noticing the issue. The IT Service Desk can be reached on internal extension 4357 or at servicedesk@brightlake.example.

## 7. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in suspension of system access and disciplinary action.
