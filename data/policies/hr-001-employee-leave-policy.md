# Employee Leave Policy (HR-001)

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE POLICY. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company. This document was created for a training exercise and does not describe any real organisation.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains the types of leave available to permanent full-time employees of Brightlake Systems and how leave is requested and approved. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis. Contractors are not covered by this policy.

## 2. Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues at 1.67 days per completed month of service.

Employees must submit annual leave requests through the HR Portal at least 7 calendar days before the first day of leave. Requests for 5 or more consecutive working days must be submitted at least 21 calendar days in advance. All annual leave requires approval from the employee's line manager.

## 3. Carry-Forward of Annual Leave

A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward into the next calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March of the following year; any carried-forward days not used by that date expire. Unused annual leave cannot be exchanged for cash, except as part of final settlement when an employee leaves the company.

## 4. Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry forward. Employees must notify their line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate from a registered doctor is required for any sick absence longer than 2 consecutive working days.

## 5. Parental Leave

Birth mothers are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Employees must have completed at least 80 days of service in the 12 months before the expected delivery date to be eligible.

Fathers and secondary caregivers are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

## 6. Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to 5 working days of paid bereavement leave following the death of an immediate family member (spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling, or grandparent).

## 7. Public Holidays

Brightlake Systems publishes a list of 12 public holidays each December for the following calendar year. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive one compensatory day off, which must be taken within 60 days.
