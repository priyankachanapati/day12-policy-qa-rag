# Employee Onboarding Policy (HR-004)

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE POLICY. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company. This document was created for a training exercise and does not describe any real organisation.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy describes the onboarding process for new permanent employees joining Brightlake Systems, from before their first day until the end of their probation period.

## 2. Before the First Day

New employees must submit the following documents through the HR Portal before their joining date: a government-issued photo ID, bank account details for payroll, and the signed offer letter. HR sends a welcome email with first-day instructions 5 working days before the joining date.

## 3. First Day

New employees attend an orientation session that starts at 09:30 on their first day. During orientation they receive their company laptop, ID badge, and system accounts.

## 4. Onboarding Buddy

Every new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy from their team. The buddy supports the new employee for their first 90 days.

## 5. Mandatory Training

New employees must complete two mandatory training courses within 14 calendar days of joining: Security Awareness Training and Code of Conduct Training. Both courses are completed through the Learning Portal.

## 6. Probation Period

The probation period for new employees is 6 months. Line managers hold structured check-in meetings with new employees at 30, 60, and 90 days after joining. Confirmation of employment is communicated in writing at the end of the probation period.
