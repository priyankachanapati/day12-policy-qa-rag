# Employee Reimbursement Policy (HR-003)

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE POLICY. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company. This document was created for a training exercise and does not describe any real organisation.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains which business expenses Brightlake Systems reimburses and how employees submit expense claims. It applies to all permanent employees and interns.

## 2. Submitting a Claim

Expense claims must be submitted through the Expense Portal within 30 calendar days of the date the expense was incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days will be rejected unless the Finance Controller approves a late submission.

An itemised receipt is required for every individual expense above USD 25. Expenses of USD 25 or less may be claimed without a receipt, provided a description of the expense is entered.

## 3. Approval and Payment

All expense claims must be approved by the employee's line manager. Claims above USD 1,000 also require approval from the department head. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll cycle.

## 4. Business Travel - Flights and Accommodation

Flights must be booked in economy class for journeys under 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for journeys of 6 hours or longer. Hotel accommodation is reimbursed up to USD 180 per night.

## 5. Business Travel - Meals

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to USD 60 per day. Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable under any circumstances.

## 6. Use of a Personal Vehicle

Employees who use their personal vehicle for approved business travel are reimbursed at USD 0.40 per kilometre. Fuel receipts are not required for mileage claims, but the start point, destination and distance must be recorded in the claim.

## 7. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursable: alcoholic beverages, traffic or parking fines, personal entertainment, in-flight purchases, and expenses for family members accompanying the employee.
