# IT Equipment Usage Policy (IT-002)

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE POLICY. Brightlake Systems is a fictional company. This document was created for a training exercise and does not describe any real organisation.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains how company-issued IT equipment, such as laptops, monitors, headsets and mobile phones, must be used, protected, and returned.

## 2. Issued Equipment

Every employee is issued a company laptop on or before their first working day. Laptops are replaced on a standard refresh cycle of every 4 years, or earlier if the device is faulty and cannot be repaired.

All company laptops use full-disk encryption, which must never be disabled.

## 3. Acceptable Use

Company equipment is provided for business use. Limited personal use, such as reading personal email during breaks, is permitted provided it does not interfere with work or breach any company policy.

## 4. Software Installation

Employees may install software only from the Brightlake Software Centre. Installing any other software requires prior approval from IT through a request raised in TechDesk, the IT ticketing system.

## 5. Removable Storage

USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops by default. Only encrypted USB drives issued by the IT department may be used.

## 6. Lost or Stolen Equipment

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours of discovering the loss. If a device is stolen, the employee must also file a police report and attach the report reference to the TechDesk ticket.

## 7. Returning Equipment

When an employee leaves Brightlake Systems, all company equipment must be returned within 5 working days of the last working day. The cost of unreturned equipment may be deducted from the employee's final settlement.

## 8. Contact

Questions about this policy should be directed to the IT Service Desk on internal extension 4357.
